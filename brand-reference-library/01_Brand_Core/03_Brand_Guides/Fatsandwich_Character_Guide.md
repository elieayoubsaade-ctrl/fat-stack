# Fatsandwich Character Guide

All characters share the same illustration DNA: hand-drawn, thick black outlines, flat color fills, slightly imperfect line quality, 90s/early-2000s cartoon energy. They are anthropomorphized food items or food-adjacent figures, each with a distinct personality and visual world.

---

## The Fat Sandwich
**File:** `templates/characters/the-fat-sandwich.jpeg` / `no-bg/the-fat-sandwich.png`
**Background:** Medium purple (#7B5EA7)
**Description:** An anthropomorphic hoagie/sub roll with a face. The bread has a criss-cross scored pattern on top (like a Dutch crunch roll). Two large round eyes with black pupils are embedded in the front of the roll. Tiny stick-like arms and legs extend from the sides and bottom. The roll is slightly tilted, giving it a casual, relaxed posture.
**Typography:** "the FAT SANDWICH" — "the" in small handwritten script above, "FAT" and "SANDWICH" in large rounded orange/red comic lettering
**Personality:** The brand mascot. Chill, self-aware, the main character of the universe.
**Use for:** Hero brand moments, main mascot applications, brand introductions

## Character Consistency Rules

When generating new images featuring any character:
1. Maintain the exact visual description above — do not modernize or over-polish
2. Keep the hand-drawn, slightly imperfect line quality
3. Use the character's signature background color as the primary background
4. Keep the character's name typography in the same style as described
5. Characters can be placed in new scenes or poses but their core design must not change
6. All characters share the same illustration universe — same line weight, same flat color approach
