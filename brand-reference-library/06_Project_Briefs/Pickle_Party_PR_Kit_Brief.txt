PRODUCT PHOTOGRAPH — PR KIT / INFLUENCER SEEDING BOX

SUBJECT
A branded press kit box, open, presented hero-style at a three-quarter
angle. The box is the invitation to "The Pickle Party," an opening
event for Fat Sandwich, a Dubai American-deli sandwich brand.

CONTENTS, arranged inside and around the open box:
- ONE large whole dill pickle, vacuum-sealed in a clear flat pouch.
  The pickle is the hero object: bumpy, deep olive-green, clearly
  visible through taut transparent film, brine and a dill sprig sealed
  in with it. It sits upright or propped at an angle so it reads
  immediately.
- A small rectangular invitation card in the brand's vivid red,
  slightly tilted, leaning against the pickle pouch.
- A folded sheet of white deli wrap paper printed with a repeating
  pattern of small hand-drawn cartoon characters, used as tissue/void
  fill, spilling slightly over the box edge.
- A red enamel pin or sticker sheet resting on the paper.
- Optional: a single wooden clothespin, as used on the brand's bags.

BRAND IDENTITY — follow exactly
- Primary red: warm vivid pillar-box red, approx #D32C22
- Secondary purple: medium violet, approx #6B3FA0
- Logo: a jagged comic-book starburst / explosion shape in purple with
  a heavy black outline, with the words FAT SANDWICH inside it in red
  rounded bubble letters, also black-outlined, slightly stacked on two
  lines. Comic, graffiti-adjacent, hand-drawn, NOT clean corporate.
- Box exterior: white with red side panels, matching the existing
  packaging system.
- Character illustrations: naive, hand-drawn, 1990s sticker-book style
  cartoon food characters in flat colour with wobbly black linework.
  Small scale, scattered, repeating.

PHOTOGRAPHY DIRECTION
- Studio product photography, single soft key light from upper left,
  gentle fill, one soft natural shadow falling to the lower right.
- Surface: brushed stainless steel worktop with fine circular scratches
  catching the light, consistent with the brand's existing product
  shots. Subtle reflection under the box.
- Background: clean, slightly out of focus, dark neutral, so the red
  and purple pop.
- Lens feel: 50mm, shallow but not extreme depth of field. The pickle
  and the logo both in sharp focus.
- Colour: rich and saturated but not artificial. Real food, real
  cardboard, real plastic film. Visible paper texture and a slight
  crease or two — this should look like an object that exists, not a
  render.

MOOD
Playful, loud, a little irreverent. Premium in construction but
scrappy in attitude — a proper deli, not a luxury brand. It should
look like something a food creator would immediately photograph.

FORMAT
Square, 1:1, high resolution. Leave a little clean negative space at
the top right for later copy overlay.

AVOID
No hands or people. No excessive gloss or plastic-looking CGI sheen.
No stock-photo styling. No lens flare. No confetti or balloons. Do not
invent additional logos, badges or seals. Do not add long paragraphs
of body text.
